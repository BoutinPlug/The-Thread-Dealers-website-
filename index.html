03 / How it works

Four steps. We carry the hard parts.

See the full process
  1. 1

    Tell us the purpose

    Staff wear, an event, a team or a brand launch. Describe the goal and who will be wearing it.

  2. 2

    Start from a proven garment

    We recommend the right piece and weight from the Core Collection, so you never have to guess at fabrics.

  3. 3

    Approve the details

    You sign off on the proof, sizes and placement. We pick the best way to apply your logo, print or embroidery.

  4. 4

    Delivered and worn again

    Most orders reach your door 6 to 8 weeks after approval. Then it goes into rotation.
